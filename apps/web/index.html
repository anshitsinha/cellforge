Workflows

Built around the work you close every month.

The spreadsheet you know, with the tools an analyst actually reaches for: each one is a command, not an afternoon of formulas.

Bank reconciliation

Bank to ledger in minutes, not an afternoon of VLOOKUPs.

  • Matches by reference, amount and date, with tolerances, similar descriptions and split payments.
  • Flags keying errors (swapped digits, a flipped sign) and duplicate postings, and says why two items don't match.
  • Builds the rec statement, with proposed journals for the items only the bank knows about.
  • Opens bank statements straight from PDF, with every running balance checked.

Month-end close

A trial balance that checks itself.

  • Map accounts to report lines once; next month the mapping is already there.
  • Flux analysis flags every movement over your thresholds and asks for the explanation.
  • Checks run before anything leaves: debits equal credits, every account mapped, no code twice.
  • Clean up messy ERP exports, with titles, subtotals and page footers, in one step, and repeat it next month as a recipe.
Open a sample close →

Audit tests

Journal entry testing, without a scripting language.

  • Eleven journal entry tests in one pass: unbalanced, duplicate, backdated, weekend, after hours, just below an approval limit, unusual user, keywords and more. Every entry scored and ranked.
  • Benford (first and first-two digits, with MAD and chi-square), duplicates exact, near and fuzzy, sequence gaps, stratification, Pareto and ageing.
  • Random, systematic, stratified and monetary unit samples, with the seed recorded so anyone can pick the same sample again.

Evidence and review

Every number explained. Every step signed off.

  • Tick marks, review notes, and sign-off as prepared and reviewed, on every sheet.
  • "Explain this number" traces a figure through its formulas to the rows and files it came from.
  • Link a cell to the bank letter or invoice that supports it, and check that they agree.
  • An evidence pack keeps the workbook, the fingerprints of every input and a tamper-evident log of every step, in one file.

Private AI

An AI assistant that never sees the internet.

Say what you want in plain English and see exactly what will change before anything does. The model runs on your computer, inside the browser tab. Nothing you type, and none of your data, is sent anywhere.

  • Shows its plan first. Every request is described in words, which column, which rows, before you press Do it.
  • Always undoable. Whatever it does is an ordinary step: Ctrl+Z, history and recipes work as usual.
  • Checked against your sheet. The model can only answer with the app's own actions, and each one is checked before it runs.
  • Helps where it counts. Rephrases formulas in "Explain this number", finds spelling variants of vendor names, and suggests categories for rows no rule covers.
  1. 1
    Start without a model. The ask bar already understands common requests: sort, filter, highlight, freeze, find duplicates.
  2. 2
    Get a model. Open Private AI with the Ask AI button. It suggests the model that suits your computer; press Download, or use a .gguf file you already have.
  3. 3
    Load it once. It's kept in the browser's private storage and loads by itself next time; remove it whenever you like.

Nothing is downloaded until you press Download, which names the site and the size. cellforge never calls an AI service. How the local AI works

Privacy

Client data stays where it belongs.

No server sees your files, so there is nothing to approve, nothing to breach and nothing to explain to a client.

Nothing leaves your computer

No upload, no account, no telemetry. Files are read in the browser and your work is kept in its private storage.

Works offline

After the first visit it runs with no connection at all. Install it from the browser's menu, or run it on an air-gapped machine.

Share safely

Before a file goes out, cellforge lists what's hidden in it, masks personal data on request and can save a password-protected copy.
