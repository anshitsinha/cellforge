Built for the jobs you actually do

Each one is a command, not an afternoon of formulas.

Messy exports, cleaned

Report-style exports with titles, subtotals and page footers become a clean table. (1,234.50), 1.234,56, 500 CR and any date format turn into real numbers and dates, and you see every value that didn't fit.

Bank statements from PDF

Open the bank's PDF and get the transactions, with wrapped descriptions joined, years filled in and every running balance checked page by page.

Reconciliations that explain themselves

Match ledger to bank by reference, amount and date, with tolerances, fuzzy text and split payments. Keying errors are flagged, and "why don't these match?" tells you. Then the rec statement, with proposed journals.

Audit tests in a click

Duplicates (exact, near and fuzzy), sequence gaps, Benford, journal entry tests, stratification and samples (random, systematic, monetary unit) with the seed recorded.

Evidence for every number

Tick marks, review notes and sign-off. Link a cell to the figure in the bank letter and check it agrees. "Explain this number" traces it to its source, and an evidence pack proves what was done.

Do it again next month

Every step is recorded as a recipe. Tidy it, save it, and run it on next month's files, or a whole folder, as one step you can undo.

Private by design

  1. Nothing leaves your computer

    There's no server and no account. Files are read in your browser, and your work is kept in your browser's private storage.

  2. Share safely

    Before you send a file, cellforge lists what's hidden in it (hidden rows, notes, history, personal data) and can mask personal data or save a password-protected copy.

  3. Works offline

    After the first visit it runs with no connection. Install it from the browser's menu, or build it and run it from a folder.

How it stays fast

  1. Columnar storage

    Each column is a single typed array. 100M numbers is one Float64Array, not 100M objects for the garbage collector to chase.

  2. Virtualized canvas

    The grid is one <canvas> that paints only the visible cells, so scrolling 100 rows and 100 million costs the same.

  3. Compiled formulas

    A formula filled down a column is one vector operation, compiled to a single loop over typed arrays, 6–22× faster than interpreting it, with bit-identical results. Run the benchmark in your own browser.
