Fill-down kernel benchmark

Evaluates one formula filled down N rows three ways: the tree-walking interpreter, the compiled JS kernel (f64, bit-identical to the interpreter), and a WebGPU compute shader (f32). Everything runs in this tab.

formula method rows median ms M rows/s vs interpreter notes

Method

  • Inputs A, B, C are random positive doubles (B never 0), so every row takes the fast path.
  • Each method runs once to warm up, then runs times; the median is reported.
  • The interpreter is capped at 2M rows (it's slow); its rows/s is measured on that slice.
  • JS kernel results are checked against the interpreter row-by-row on the interpreted slice (expect 0 mismatches).
  • GPU timing is end to end: f64→f32 conversion, upload, compute and readback. "compute" in notes is the dispatch alone.
  • GPU error is the max relative difference vs the f64 kernel.
