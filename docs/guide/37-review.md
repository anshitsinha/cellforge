# Review: tick marks, review notes and sign-off

## Tick marks

Select cells and choose a tick mark in the command palette (type "tick
mark"): ✓ agreed to source, F footed, CF cross-footed, R recalculated,
T traced to the ledger, V vouched, PBC provided by client, ✗ exception.
The mark is drawn small in the cell, on the side the value leaves free.
Choosing the same mark again on a marked cell removes it.

**Add a tick-mark legend sheet** adds a sheet listing every mark and what
it means, for the working paper.

## Review notes

**Add a review note…** puts a reviewer's point on a cell: "Agree to the
bank letter", "Why has this moved?". The first time, cellforge asks for your
name or initials (kept in this browser). A cell with an open review note
has a blue corner (top left); rest the pointer on it to read it.

**Clear review note (dealt with)** marks it answered, with who and when;
**Reopen review note** opens it again. Review notes, unlike ordinary notes,
stay on the record after they're cleared.

## Sign-off

**Sign off this sheet as prepared** and then **… as reviewed** record who
and when. Sign-off is refused while the sheet has open review notes, and
reviewed needs prepared first. Signed sheets show ✓ (prepared) or ✓✓
(reviewed) on their tab.

If the sheet changes after it's signed, **Review status (sign-offs, open
notes)…** says "changed since" with the number of changes, worked out from
the evidence log. The panel lists every sheet with its reference, its
sign-offs and its open review notes (click one to go to it).

## Workpaper references

**Workpaper reference…** gives the sheet a reference like `B-3`; it shows
on the tab and in the evidence pack's summary, along with each sheet's
sign-offs and open notes.

Everything here is saved with the workbook and each change is one undo
step.
